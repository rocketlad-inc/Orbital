Why this deploys clean: one solar system in world-space, one camera. Opening a menu is the camera flying to a body — the same disc grows from map dot to upper-limb horizon (no crossfade, no second planet). Menu chrome resolves in only at arrival; map furniture fades on the exact inverse. All world-space UI is sized as fractions of the body radius. The suite below is the v2 spec, runnable in place.
