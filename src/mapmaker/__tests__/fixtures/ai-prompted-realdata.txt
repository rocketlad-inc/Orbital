Here is TRAPPIST-1 for seven players. Real data used: the planets' true orbit ratios (b through h, from 0.0115 to 0.0619 AU), real sizes scaled so Earth = 3 (b 1.12, c 1.10, d 0.79, e 0.92, f 1.05, g 1.13, h 0.76 Earth radii), and the real order of surfaces: scorched b and c, the temperate d to g, an icy h. The whole real system is only about 0.06 AU across, which is smaller than the game's 60-unit minimum orbit. So I scaled all orbits by one factor (b at 100 units), which keeps the true spacing. The sun is a tiny, dim red dwarf, so I used the minimum size and mass the game allows. The real system has no known moons, belt or outer planets. I added a few clearly labelled hypothetical ones so every player has worlds to fight over.
